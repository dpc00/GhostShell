**Cross-cutting risks (measured or known):** timer granularity ~15 ms (Windows) means echo latency; private asyncio calls (`_proactor.send`, `_set_running_loop`, `_run_once`); a CPU-heavy coroutine stalls the editor; the loop must be stopped and pipes closed on plugin unload/reload (a half-loaded reload bit us earlier today: new `ai_terminal.py` with old `tsp_view.py`); `_Pty` (non-detachable) cannot use overlapped I/O on its anonymous pipes. UNTESTED: end-of-stream (the read side did not report close within 10 s after `exit`), resize through the ctl pipe, many tabs at once, restart survival, `wait_for_handle`.
