- ROOT CAUSE of the 580-row buffer FOUND 2026-10-14 (his hint: trimming is inhibited under heavy output): _set_auto_follow mirrors follow onto screen.trim_paused (screen.py: while paused, old scrollback is kept, uncapped, and the backlog is trimmed at once when it resumes). The render loop's user-scroll detector (the _run call sites that set follow False; 21 of 27 traced False flips) disengages follow on viewport moves that are not the user; with scroll_manipulation_enabled false (Claude profile) nothing re-engages it until his next keypress, so trimming stops, the tab grows to about 580 rows, and the keypress releases the backlog in one redraw = the jump. FIX (uncommitted, loaded live): in _set_auto_follow, only pause trimming when scroll_manipulation_enabled is true (setting trim_pause_needs_follow_machinery, default true). Plus the earlier _clamp_after_shrink as a safety net. Live check right after: Claude tab auto_follow False (stray), trim_paused False, history 300 = cap, 337 rows. Not fixed: why the detector disengages follow on non-user moves. If he sees a jump again, read the newest dump.
